Le Studio

Parle français, tous les jours.

The French speaking coach that finds the mistakes you keep making

Le Studio listens to you speak French and tells you, in plain English, exactly what you keep getting wrong — then shows you honest proof of when you've fixed it. It's built around the WJEC, AQA and Edexcel GCSE and A-level speaking exams, and runs entirely in your browser. Your progress stays on your device by default; an account is optional and the AI feedback works without one.

  • Speak, and find out what's really going wrong — hold a conversation in French with an AI partner that corrects your grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation as you go, and names the specific mistakes you repeat rather than a vague score.
  • Exam Speaking Mode — timed role-play, photo-card and general-conversation tasks in the shape of the real WJEC, AQA and Edexcel papers, marked against band descriptors with the time shortfall shown first. Indicative practice papers, clearly not official ones — check your board for the real thing.
  • A mistakes-fixed timeline that won't flatter you — every weakness is tracked from the moment it's first spotted, through repair, an unseen retest and a delayed re-check. One correct answer never counts as mastery, and if evidence is missing it stays missing.
  • Vocabulary — 2,637 entries across 5 curriculum packs, scheduled with FSRS spaced repetition, with IPA and text-to-speech.
  • Grammar — 60 interactive CEFR-tagged topics with drills, sentence building and scored quizzes, plus conjugation tables.
  • Also — dictée and 19 listening tracks, graded readers, a pronunciation coach, writing feedback and conjugation drills. Installable PWA, works offline.
