Le Studio — French Speaking Practice

A free, all-in-one studio for practising French — speaking, listening, reading and writing — with an AI conversation partner. Everything runs in your browser: no backend, no account, and your progress stays on your device.

  • Roleplay Arena — voice conversations across 40 scenarios (bistro, hotel, taxi, doctor, bank, job interview, free talk…) with per-turn corrections and scored feedback.
  • Learning Path — goal-based roadmaps: 12 units and 60 lessons per goal, an adaptive CEFR placement test (A1–C2), scored checkpoints and knowledge stats.
  • Vocabulary — 42 themed flashcard packs (520+ rich cards) with SM-2 spaced repetition and a ~2,000-word offline dictionary with IPA and text-to-speech.
  • Skills — pronunciation coaching, dictée, hands-free audio course, numbers rapid-fire, accent trainer, translation drills, mini-podcasts, graded readers and classics.
  • Grammar — 25 interactive CEFR-tagged topics with drills, sentence building and scored quizzes, plus conjugation tables.
  • Culture & motivation — cultural notes, XP, streaks with vacation mode, habit tracker, analytics and weekly reports. Installable PWA, works offline.
