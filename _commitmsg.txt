fix(gamification): the hub shows the learner's own game

The Gamification Hub was a fiction. It carried a fixed learner (47 sessions,
523 words, a 12-day streak, 250 coins) and invented the rest: four rivals
with random XP, a random rank out of an invented field of 15,000 players, a
percentile derived from that rank, a random challenge picked on every
render, and a "Complete Challenge" button with nothing behind it.

The studio already has a game. The hub now shows that one:

- Badges: the fourteen real achievements, each with how far along the
  learner is ("4 / 5 sessions"), read from the same stats the unlock
  condition is written against.
- Challenges: today's three real challenges, with a button that really
  claims them.
- League: the same weekly league the profile shows, against seeded
  pace-setters - never real people.
- Boosts: the streak freeze, at the app's real price (150 coins). The
  double-XP boost and the instant review pack were never part of the game,
  so they are not on offer.

src/lib/gamification.js is removed: it existed only to feed this panel
invented numbers, and nothing else imported it.

The badge conditions, the stats they are measured against, and the rules
for handing out what has been earned now live in one place each
(src/lib/game.js, src/lib/gameStats.js, src/lib/gameGrants.js), so the
profile and the hub cannot disagree about how far along the learner is.
